What the functions do

Join another campaign

Ask the other GM for their join key. After your character joins, that campaign appears in your campaign list because you have a PC on it.

  1. Open Characters.
  2. Right-click your character and choose Join other's campaign.
  3. Enter the key and confirm. The character must be one you own.

The GM sees your character on the party bench. You see the campaign in your list.

Joining the campaign is not the same as being in a fight. The Encounters rail stays empty for you until the GM adds your character to that encounter. Prep fights you are not on stay hidden.

A character already in another campaign has to move. Adding them to a new key takes them off the previous campaign.
