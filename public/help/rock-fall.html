What the functions do

Rock fall

Everyone in the fight, allies and enemies, must attempt a Reflex save against falling rock. This check is not an Amba challenge, so it is ad hoc.

  1. Open the campaign encounter as the GM.
  2. Open the Dice tab.
  3. Set Who to include to Both if you want one roll for the whole side, or leave it and roll people one at a time.
  4. Set Challenge to Ad hoc.
  5. Title: Rock fall.
  6. Check: Reflex (Saves).
  7. DC: the number you called for the save.
  8. Right-click an ally or an enemy. Choose Roll this challenge if Reflex is already the toolbar check, or open Check, then Saves, then Reflex.
  9. Repeat for each combatant who must save. Each result is written to the roll log.

Players on that fight see ally rows in full and living enemies as initials plus initiative. They see ally rolls in the log. Enemy names stay abbreviated on their screen.
