What the functions do

GM view

You see everything a player sees, plus the rest of the fight.

  • Every encounter in the campaign, including fights no player character is on yet.
  • Full enemy names, defenses, hit points, and dead enemies.
  • Edits on the roster: add or remove combatants, change initiative and hit points, open any sheet.
  • Character sheets for the party, including characters you do not own.
  • On Dice, choose Enemies, Allies, or Both, and whether a roll is Private or Public.

Players still see abbreviated living enemies on the same encounter. Hiding a name on their screen does not remove that creature from yours.
