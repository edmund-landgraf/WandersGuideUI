What the functions do

Player view

On a campaign encounter you see the party in full and living enemies in short form.

Allies

Every player character and every ally creature shows name, initiative, defenses, hit points, and conditions. They stay on the grid at 0 HP.

Enemies

A living enemy shows initials, initiative, and conditions. Defenses and hit points are hidden. You cannot open the enemy. A creature with no stored hit points still counts as alive. At 0 HP or below, that enemy drops off your grid. The others keep their numbers, so Mudjaw Lurkbloom (2) stays ML (2) after (1) and (3) are gone. Reefclaw 1 shows as R1.

Sheets

You can edit your own character. Other player characters and ally creatures are read only. The game master can still edit those sheets.

The round log shows ally turns. A round that only had enemies is omitted.
