Campaigns

A campaign you create is one you GM. Other people join it with the join key.

  1. From the workspace, create a campaign. Your account is prepared first so the campaign has an owner.
  2. Open that campaign’s settings.
  3. Use Reveal join key. The key stays hidden until you reveal it, then you can copy it.
  4. Send the key to players. They attach one of their characters with Join other's campaign.

Campaign settings are only available to the game master. Players see the campaign after one of their characters has joined it.
