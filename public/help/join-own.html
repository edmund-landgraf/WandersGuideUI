What the functions do

Join your own campaign

Owning a campaign does not put your character on it. Assign the character the same way a player would join.

  1. Open Characters.
  2. Right-click the character and choose Join my campaign.
  3. Pick one of the campaigns you own. If you do not own one yet, create it from Campaigns first.

That character is now a member. It shows on your party bench. It is still not in a fight until you add it to an encounter.
