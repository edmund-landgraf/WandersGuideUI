Reference

What the functions do

Run the table from one workspace

Characters, campaigns, and encounters live in the updated workspace. A campaign shows up for you once one of your characters has joined it. An encounter shows up once that character is on the fight.

Recommended path

01

Characters

Own a character, then open it from the Characters page.

 02

Campaigns

Create a campaign you run and copy the join key.

 03

Join your own campaign

Put your PC on the campaign you GM.

 04

Join another campaign

Use another GM’s key. Their campaign then appears in your list.

 05

Encounters

Open a fight your character is on.

 06

Player view

Full allies. Living enemies as initials and initiative.

 07

GM view

The same fight, with every enemy and every edit.

 08

Challenges

Amba challenges on the encounter, or an ad hoc check.

 09

Rock fall

Walk through a Reflex save for allies and enemies.
