Encounters

Open a campaign you belong to. The Encounters rail lists the fights you are allowed to see.

  • If you GM the campaign, every encounter in it is listed.
  • If you are a player, an encounter is listed only when one of your characters is on that fight.

Right-click the Encounters heading or an encounter name for the rail menu (create, rename, delete — the GM manages the list). Opening an encounter loads combat. With no encounter selected, the GM still has a New button. Combat and dice controls stay hidden until an encounter is open. Adding a bench character to combat also waits for a selected encounter.

Ally names and hit points come from the live party, so a teammate’s sheet stays current instead of a snapshot taken when they were added. Campaign art can sit behind the encounter. Campaign notes stay on the GM side of the workspace.
