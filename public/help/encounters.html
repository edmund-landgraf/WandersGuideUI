What the functions do

Encounters

Open a campaign you belong to. The Encounters rail lists the fights you are allowed to see.

  • If you GM the campaign, every encounter in it is listed.
  • If you are a player, an encounter is listed only when one of your characters is on that fight.

Right-click the Encounters heading or an encounter name for the rail menu (create, rename, delete — the GM manages the list). Opening an encounter loads combat.

Ally names and hit points come from the live party, so a teammate’s sheet stays current instead of a snapshot taken when they were added.
