What the functions do

Challenges

Open the encounter and switch to Dice. The Challenge menu reads challenges stored on that encounter by AdventureMakerByAct. If the encounter has none, the menu says No challenges.

From Amba

Pick a challenge. Its title and DC fill the toolbar, and the check follows the challenge (a skill such as Perception, or a save such as Reflex). Degree text from the challenge is attached to the roll result.

Ad hoc

Choose Ad hoc when you need a check that is not on the encounter. Type the title, set the DC, and pick the save or skill yourself. That clears any selected Amba challenge. Players stay on ad hoc; selecting an Amba challenge is for the GM.

Right-click a combatant

  • Roll this challenge uses the check or Amba challenge already set in the toolbar.
  • Check opens Senses, Saves, Ability, and Skill so you can roll that combatant against the current DC.
  • Challenge lists the Amba challenges on this encounter. It appears for the GM when the encounter has at least one.

Set a DC before a check will roll. Rolls are saved on the encounter for people who are on the fight. A player can change rolls they started. The roster stays with the GM.
