What the functions do

The home page lists these calls. Each one is a small piece of play. The launcher marks one up when the server answers.

They line up in the order you play. First the account has to exist. Then a character joins a campaign, which is what puts that campaign on your list and your PC on the GM’s bench. The party and the encounters load from that membership: the GM sees every fight, and you see only the ones your character is on. During the fight the GM adds or removes characters, rolls initiative, and edits a combatant; anyone on the fight can save dice they started. Notes follow the campaign’s sharing flag. Export is separate: it writes a character you own.

CallWhat you get
wgui-ext-ensure-public-userMakes sure your account exists before you create a campaign.
wgui-ext-join-campaignAttaches your character with a join key. That membership is why the campaign then appears for you, and why the GM sees your PC on the bench.
wgui-ext-find-campaign-charactersLoads the live party so allies show current names and hit points.
wgui-ext-find-encounterLoads campaign encounters. The GM gets every encounter. You get only the ones your character is on.
wgui-ext-patch-encounter-diceSaves dice checks and the roll log for people on the fight. Players can change rolls they started. The roster stays with the GM.
wgui-ext-patch-encounter-combatantSets initiative, hit points, temporary hit points, or conditions on one combatant. The GM only.
wgui-ext-add-encounter-characterAdds a joined character to the fight without replacing the whole list. The GM only.
wgui-ext-remove-encounter-characterRemoves a player character from the fight the same way. The GM only.
wgui-ext-roll-initiativeRolls initiative on the server for empty values or for everyone listed, then appends one log row. A second browser cannot overwrite the roll. The GM only.
wgui-ext-patch-campaign-notesUpdates shared campaign notes when a player is allowed to edit them.
wgui-ext-find-campaign-notesReturns the notes the sharing flag allows.
wgui-export-characterExports your character as JSON or PDF.
